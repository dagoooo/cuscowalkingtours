Top 10 Things to Do in Cusco

Nov 2, 2025 | By Waldo Peredo

Cusco is full of history, culture, and amazing places to explore. Here are the top 10 must-see sights during your walking tour:

1. Plaza de Armas

The historic center of Cusco, surrounded by beautiful colonial buildings and a vibrant atmosphere.

2. Sacsayhuamán

An incredible Inca fortress with giant stone walls and panoramic views of the city.

3. Qoricancha (Temple of the Sun)

Once the most important temple in the Inca Empire, now combined with the Santo Domingo convent.

4. San Pedro Market

A colorful market where you can try local food and buy souvenirs.

Tips for Visitors

  • Take it slow to adjust to Cusco’s high altitude.
  • Wear comfortable walking shoes.
  • Try local food and drinks at the markets.
